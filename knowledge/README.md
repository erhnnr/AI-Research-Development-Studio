# Knowledge Layer

Version: 0.1
Status: Active
Last Updated: 2026-07-27

---

## Purpose

The Knowledge layer stores accumulated learning produced by the Studio.

It preserves:
- research findings
- decisions
- experiments
- lessons learned
- validated and invalidated assumptions

---

## Principle

Knowledge is not an archive only.

It is the Studio's memory layer.

Information generated from projects, validation results, and research
should be captured here so future decisions are made from accumulated
knowledge rather than starting from zero.

---

## Structure

Future knowledge categories may include:

- research_notes
- lessons_learned
- decisions
- experiments
- market_insights
